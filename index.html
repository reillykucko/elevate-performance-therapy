What I Treat With

Three tools. One goal: get you moving without pain.

Every session is built from the same core toolkit, applied differently depending on what your body needs that day.

Modality — Hands-On

Manual Therapy

Joint mobilization, soft-tissue work, and guided stretching to restore range of motion and quiet down irritated tissue — no two sessions follow the same script.

  • Joint mobilization & manipulation
  • Myofascial release
  • Assisted stretching & PNF techniques
  • Post-treatment movement retraining
Modality — Trigger Point

Dry Needling

Thin filament needles placed directly into tight, overactive muscle to release trigger points, reduce guarding, and speed up recovery between training blocks.

  • Targeted trigger-point release
  • Reduced muscle guarding & tension
  • Often paired with manual therapy same-session
  • Single-use, sterile needles
Modality — Myofascial Decompression

Cupping

Negative-pressure cups lift and decompress fascia and muscle, improving local blood flow and mobility — a strong complement to manual work on chronically tight areas.

  • Static & dynamic (gliding) cupping
  • Improved tissue mobility & circulation
  • Well-suited to lats, quads & calves
  • Often used pre- or post-training

Not sure which combination fits your body? That's exactly what the first consult is for — I build the plan around your goals, not a fixed protocol.
